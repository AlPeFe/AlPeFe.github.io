// about

what i do

I'm a 32-year-old backend developer based in Barcelona, working at OriginalSoft - the people behind GAM, a platform for patient transport and emergency healthcare. I build the product end to end: the mobile app the crew carries (GamDroid), the APIs behind it, and the integrations that glue everything together.

The interesting part is the integration layer. I connect public services - SEM, SESCAM, SOS Navarra, Sergas, Osakidetza - with private players like mutuas, insurers and telemedicine providers (Ibermutua, Sanitas, ASISA, Teladoc...), plus telemetry and fleet systems. Different protocols, different formats, one system that makes them talk to each other.

Years of building APIs and systems with .NET have taught me that good software is boring software - predictable, testable, and secure by default. On the side I'm working through cybersecurity (Google Cybersecurity cert and SC-900 done, Security+ next) and local AI: self-hosted models, Ollama, agents that actually do something useful. Big believer in FOSS - my data lives on my hardware and my tools don't phone home.

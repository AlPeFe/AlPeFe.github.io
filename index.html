What I do

I'm a 32-year-old backend developer based in Barcelona. Years of building APIs and systems with .NET have taught me that good software is boring software — predictable, testable, and secure by default.

Lately I've been going deep on cybersecurity and AI. Security-first isn't a buzzword for me: Google Cybersecurity cert and SC-900 are done, Security+ is next. On the AI side I'm all about self-hosted models, local LLMs with Ollama, and building agents that actually do something useful.

Big believer in FOSS and self-hosting. My data lives on my hardware, my services run in my homelab, and my tools don't phone home.

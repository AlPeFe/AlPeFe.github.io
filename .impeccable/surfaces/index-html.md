# Surface brief — alpefe.me

**Scope:** four static pages in one repository — `index.html` (P100) plus `projects/index.html` (P101), `certs/index.html` (P102) and `about/index.html` (P103). Shared system in `assets/site.css`, `assets/fonts/` and the generated `assets/tube-map.png`.
**Visitor mode:** Persuade on the landing (the visitor decides and acts); Read on the three subpages.

## Audience, job, action
Peers, developers and recruiters arriving from a handle, a repository, a CV or a chat. Visits last seconds, on a phone at least as often as on a desktop. Job: who is this and what does he actually do. Primary action on the landing: open the work — the green `101 proyectos` row, the first numbered row under the identity. Secondary: write.

## Proof and content
Real repositories with real descriptions: nine open, seven closed, and the closed ones explained by what they do and never by their stack. Two real certifications whose images, dates and credential identifiers still wait on the owner. A dated log whose dates are the repositories' own last-change dates and say so.

## Constraints
No build step, no framework, no JavaScript at all. Fonts self-hosted. Deliberately minimal personal disclosure: no location, no legal name, no portrait, no blog. The phone rotates the glass to a taller panel so nothing is trapped inside a fixed-height screen.

## Chosen direction
The carrier the owner pinned after the first build: not a 1998 homepage on a flat page, but **the page as what a 4:3 phosphor monitor shows** — a teletext service of one person. Dark room, curved glass, a numbered page index, a dated log, four fastext keys along the bottom, and a block-mosaic wordmark set in the character grid.

**Memorable moment:** the signal is bent, not painted. A generated radial displacement map warps the page so that straight rules bow and verticals splay, exactly like glass that is not flat — and the wobble is real geometry that costs one 3 KB asset and no JavaScript.

## Unresolved decisions
The X and LinkedIn handles. Whether the email stays public. The certificate images, dates and credential identifiers. Whether the retired 88×31 GIFs return on a links page. Whether the project curation stays at sixteen.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The site is not a page that looks like a monitor; it is what the monitor shows. A teletext service of exactly one person: a numbered index, a dated log, four keyed destinations, all set in a character grid inside curved glass. It refuses the dark portfolio page with neon accents laid out on a flat surface — the flatness is the thing it will not have.

**OWN-WORLD.** A dark room (`#03050a`) holding a 4:3 tube: a plastic bezel with a green pilot light, and behind the glass a page in the eight teletext inks — none of them pure — over a phosphor black (`#04060b`). Yellow (`#e8d84a`) is the page bar and every quantity; cyan (`#3ce8ff`) is structure, rules and body links; green (`#3ce86a`) is the state that means open; red (`#ff5442`) the one that means closed; magenta (`#ff58c8`) appears only as the magenta route key; blue (`#3c6cff`) is never text, only a fill. Prose runs in a dimmed phosphor white. Faces: DotGothic16, a 16px bitmap gothic, sets every word including the block-mosaic wordmark drawn out of `█`; Doto sets the page number and every numeral. Above the signal sit three vertical RGB aperture stripes at 3px and horizontal scanlines at 3px, then a tube vignette, a glass reflection and a slow vertical-retrace band.

**STORY.** The visitor understands in one line who this is and what he builds, believes it because the rows carry real dates and real repository names, and takes one of two actions: opens the work or writes.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** One 4:3 tube centred in a dark room. Inside the curved glass, safe from the edge: a yellow page bar reading `P100 ALPEFE.ME` with the site's honesty claim on the right; the six-character block-mosaic wordmark in phosphor white; one line of claim; a rule; four numbered destination rows in yellow-and-cyan with their facts on the right; a tagged log of six dated changes with open and closed states; a contact pair; and pinned to the bottom, the four fastext keys in red, green, yellow and cyan.

**FORM.** A 4:3 phosphor tube showing a teletext page. The carrier the owner pinned, carried forward from the roll's grounded candidate 5 as a translation of its materials, not a replacement of its identity. `seed:ed42a1120d78469f`

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

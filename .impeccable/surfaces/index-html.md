# Surface brief — alpefe.me

**Scope:** four static pages in one repository — the landing `index.html` plus `projects/index.html`, `certs/index.html` and `about/index.html`. Shared system in `assets/site.css` and `assets/fonts/`.
**Visitor mode:** Persuade on the landing (the visitor decides and acts); Read on the three subpages.

## Audience, job, action
Peers, developers and recruiters arriving from a handle, a repository, a CV or a chat. Visits last seconds, on a phone at least as often as on a desktop. Job: who is this and what does he actually do. Primary action on the landing: open the work — `[ 01 ] proyectos`, the only reserved-amber element on the site. Secondary: write.

## Proof and content
Real repositories with real descriptions: nine open, seven closed, and the closed ones explained by what they do and never by their stack. Two real certifications whose images, dates and credential identifiers still wait on the owner. A dated log whose dates are the repositories' own last-change dates and say so.

## Constraints
No build step, no framework, no JavaScript at all. Fonts self-hosted. Deliberately minimal personal disclosure: no location, no legal name, no portrait, no blog. Reserved amber is spent once and only on the landing.

## Chosen direction
Índice Webring — the direction the roll assigned (position 5 of the ordered grounded list): the site as a 1998 personal homepage, with an `index of /` header, an 88×31 plate rack as the only navigation, a ring bar that walks the site's own four pages, and a dated log of what actually changed.

**Memorable moment:** the plates behave like two ink layers printed slightly out of register. Hover separates them, pressing collapses them flush, and the label jitters in two steps like a console cursor.

## Unresolved decisions
The X and LinkedIn handles. Whether the email stays public. The certificate images, dates and credential identifiers. Whether the retired 88×31 GIFs return on a links page. Whether the project curation stays at sixteen.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The landing is an index, not a brochure: a directory listing of one person, where the navigation is a rack of 88×31 plates and every claim is a row carrying a date. It refuses the category's centred hero sitting over a row of equal teaser cards.

**OWN-WORLD.** Two inks own every mark — hot magenta (`#ff3d8b`) for structure, rules, plate borders and headings; acid green (`#7dff3d`) for state, labels and quantities. One impure violet-white stock (`#e8e0ff`) carries prose. One reserved amber (`#ffcc33`) is spent a single time on the landing, on the primary plate, and never on the subpages. The ground is a violet-black (`#0b0912`) under a 4px dot tile, CRT scanlines and a tube vignette, inside a 1px magenta bezel with a dotted inner line. Faces: Doto, a dot matrix, for the wordmark and for every numeral; DotGothic16, a 16px bitmap gothic, for every other word.

**STORY.** The visitor understands in one line who this is and what he builds, believes it because the rows carry real dates and real repository names, and takes one of two actions: opens the work or writes.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Inside a 1px magenta bezel inset 12px: a top bar naming `index of / alpefe.me` and the site's honesty claim; the dot-matrix wordmark at roughly 109px with a magenta and green chromatic split and `· polaper ·` beneath it; a two-sentence claim held between hairlines; the rack of three 88×31 plates at their true 2.84:1 proportion with a numbered band under each, the first outlined in the reserved amber; and then the ring bar naming the site's four pages with the current one marked in solid green.

**FORM.** 88×31 plate rack as an index page. Grounded candidate 5 of 7, dealt by the roll. `seed:ed42a1120d78469f`

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
